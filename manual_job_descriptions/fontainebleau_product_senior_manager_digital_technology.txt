POSITION OVERVIEW

The Digital Technology team at Fontainebleau Las Vegas is seeking a Sr Product Manager for MarTech. This person will serve as the Digital Technology point of contact for our marketing teams, aligning tech services with the strategic goals of the organization. This role involves developing and implementing a comprehensive digital strategy and vision, leading development teams, managing projects, and fostering a culture of collaboration and high performance. The ideal candidate will bring deep expertise in marketing technology (MarTech), including Customer Data Platform (CDP), digital campaign orchestration, and product management creating web, mobile, and other digital experiences.

ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

The following and other duties may be assigned as necessary:

Serve as the primary Digital Tech liaison for marketing business partners, aligning tech services with their strategic objectives
Develop and implement a comprehensive digital strategy and vision for one or more business portfolios at Fontainebleau Las Vegas
Lead tech teams in the execution of projects and the management of services, promoting a collaborative and high-performance culture
Identify and leverage opportunities for digital transformation and innovation within business processes
Oversee tech projects from inception to completion, ensuring timely delivery within scope and budget
Coordinate with project teams, stakeholders, and vendors to ensure successful project outcomes
Partner with service management process owners to provide service excellence, including incident, problem, and change management
Establish and maintain strong relationships with business stakeholders, managing expectations and providing regular updates
Collaborate with marketing and loyalty teams to define and implement MarTech solutions that enhance customer engagement and personalization
Drive the integration and optimization of Customer Data Platform (CDP) to unify customer profiles and enable targeted marketing efforts
Support the execution and measurement of digital marketing campaigns across channels including email, SMS, push notifications, and paid media
Lead product management efforts for web, mobile web, and mobile app platforms, including user experience design, feature prioritization, and roadmap development
Translate business needs into detailed product requirements and collaborate with design and development teams to deliver high-quality digital experiences
