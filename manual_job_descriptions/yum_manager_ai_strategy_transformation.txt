Manager, AI Strategy & Transformation

This role will translate business problems into scalable AI solutions and ensure the responsible and measurable deployment of AI across the organization. As a highly structured thinker and influential collaborator, you will help scale AI literacy, champion new tools and capabilities, and guide end-to-end automation initiatives that improve productivity, decision quality, and operational speed. This role operates with high autonomy, works across diverse stakeholders, and delivers outcomes that meaningfully shape Yum!’s AI maturity. The ideal candidate is a collaborative, action-oriented leader who can translate emerging AI capabilities into practical solutions and engaging learning experiences, influence stakeholders across functions, and drive measurable outcomes at scale.


Enterprise AI Strategy & Program Leadership

Lead the development, launch, and ongoing optimization of Yum!’s enterprise AI initiatives. 
Develop and execute enterprise AI literacy programs through our AI Academy to drive AI education, engagement, and adoption across the organization. 
Scale enterprise agents and accelerate the shift toward workflow-integrated, action-oriented automation that streamlines processes across the business.  
Translate enterprise AI priorities and business problems into practical learning pathways, scalable solutions, and resources. 
Identify opportunities and risks across the AI roadmap and develop contingency plans to support adoption, impact, and responsible deployment. 
AI Adoption & Change Enablement

Build local communities of practice and train AI champions who can advocate for, model, and scale AI adoption across Yum!. 
Drive awareness, engagement, and adoption through clear communications, stakeholder updates, and targeted enablement campaigns. 
Influence stakeholders across functions and brands by communicating complex AI concepts in a clear, practical, and business-relevant way. 
Represent the Enterprise AI Strategy & Transformation workstream as a key point of contact for internal stakeholders and external partners as needed. 
Cross-Functional Partnership & Influence

Partner with experts across AI, Learning, Analytics, Product, Engineering, and brand teams to integrate AI tools into daily workflows. 
Influence stakeholders across functions and brands by communicating complex AI concepts in a clear, practical, and business-relevant way. 
Prepare updates, recommendations, and insights for senior leaders on Enterprise AI progress, risks, adoption trends, and business impact. 

Required Qualifications

8+ years of experience in digital transformation, AI strategy, learning technology, product management, change management, or related fields. 
Experience leading cross-functional initiatives with measurable business, adoption, or productivity outcomes. 
Strong understanding of current AI trends, tools, and practical enterprise use cases. 
Experience partnering with product, engineering, learning, analytics, or business teams to design and scale new programs or platforms. 
Proven ability to influence stakeholders, communicate with senior leaders, and simplify complex topics for non-technical audiences. 
Strong program leadership skills, including roadmap development, prioritization, stakeholder management, and performance tracking. 
Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field or equivalent work experience. 
Preferred Qualifications

Experience building or scaling learning, training, enablement, or capability-building products. 
Experience launching enterprise adoption programs, communities of practice, or champion networks. 
Familiarity with AI productivity tools, generative AI platforms, prompt design, responsible AI practices, or AI literacy programs.
