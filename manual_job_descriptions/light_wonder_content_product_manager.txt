Light & Wonder Content Product Manager

Light & Wonder's digital team leads the global iGaming market, providing game content and software for online gaming providers.

The Content Product Manager is responsible for day-to-day management and execution of Light & Wonder's games portfolio across North America, working with internal studios, Commercial, and Product functions. Reporting to the Director, Product Management Games, this role ensures studio roadmaps are well-defined, commercially optimized, and executed to a high standard.

The role operates at the intersection of Product, Studios, and Commercial teams, driving product planning, delivery, and go-to-market execution. It requires a proactive, data-driven approach with an understanding of game development and market dynamics to identify opportunities, solve problems, and maximize portfolio performance.

Responsibilities:
- Own day-to-day management of the North American studio roadmap across studios.
- Ensure a balanced, high-performing portfolio aligned to regional market needs and global content strategy.
- Prioritize game releases and initiatives based on commercial impact, player value, and strategic fit.
- Assess portfolio performance and optimize sequencing, mix, and focus.
- Manage the end-to-end product lifecycle from concept through post-release optimization.
- Work with studio teams to ensure games are developed in line with product management specifications and quality expectations.
- Align Product, Creative, and Studio teams through the game development lifecycle.
- Identify and resolve delivery risks, gaps, and inefficiencies.
- Lead GTM planning for key game releases, ensuring positioning, messaging, and coordinated execution.
- Own and expand Sales Enablement programs for Commercial teams.
- Equip Commercial teams with tools, insights, and narratives to sell the portfolio.
- Partner with Commercial teams to align product plans with market opportunities and customer needs.
- Support regional activation of global content strategy.
- Work with Creative Studios, Game Studios, Commercial, and Product teams.
- Ensure alignment between central strategy and regional market requirements.
- Use performance data, market insights, and player behavior to inform decisions and prioritization.
- Monitor KPIs across the portfolio and identify opportunities for optimization and growth.
- Drive continuous improvement through testing, analysis, and iteration.
- Partner with analytics teams to generate actionable insights for roadmap and GTM decisions.
- Develop expertise in game design, mechanics, production processes, North American markets, player preferences, and regulatory considerations.
- Act as a trusted product expert for internal stakeholders.

Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, Game Design, or related field.
- 5+ years experience in product management, portfolio management, or content strategy, ideally in gaming, iGaming, entertainment, or media.
- Experience managing a product roadmap or portfolio across stakeholders with accountability for commercial outcomes.
- Experience working with or within game development studios and understanding production pipelines, creative processes, and delivery timelines.
