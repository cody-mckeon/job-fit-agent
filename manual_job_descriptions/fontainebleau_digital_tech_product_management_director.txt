POSITION OVERVIEW:

The Director, Digital Tech Product Management provides strategic leadership for a team of Product Managers responsible for product strategy, portfolio management, and business partnerships that deliver complex, cross-functional technology solutions. This role combines strong people leadership and coaching capabilities with deep expertise in product management, Agile methodologies, and software development practices. The position is accountable for developing talent, driving operational excellence, establishing product management standards and frameworks, and ensuring the successful delivery of business and technology outcomes. The Executive Director serves as a key liaison across business, technology, and vendor organizations, championing a data-driven approach to product strategy, prioritization, and execution while fostering a culture of collaboration, innovation, and continuous improvement.


ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:

The following and other duties may be assigned as necessary:

Lead, mentor, and develop a team of Product Managers, fostering a culture of growth, accountability, and collaboration
Provide goals, coaching, performance feedback, and career development support tailored to individual and team needs
Promote a culture of continuous learning, improvement, and innovation
Work with business stakeholders to identify business goals and work with stakeholders and vendors to formulate product strategies across multiple LOBs
Create and manage product roadmaps supporting product strategies and ensure cross-roadmap alignment
Manage Technology Product portfolio, creating a holistic POV and drive product changes to improve/optimize the product portfolio
Employ product management best practices, responsible for product roadmaps and requirements while utilizing Agile (Hybrid), Scrum, and SDLC methodologies
Partner with engineering, product, and business leaders to aligns business and product strategies to prioritize initiatives
Use Jira and Confluence for product tracking, documentation, and collaboration
Also work as a hands-on Product Manager setting the example and standards for the Product Management team
Establish and refine Product Management frameworks, presentations, tools and communications
Monitor product health and business objectives using KPIs and data-driven insights
Identify and resolve product challenges working closely with vendors, business partners, and the technology teams
Create a culture of clear, quality, concise, and timely communications, while strategically using the various communication tools, including email, chat, wikis, gantt charts, and slideware
Serve as a key liaison between business partners and senior leadership, ensuring alignment on priorities and outcomes
Ensure cross-functional collaboration and decision-making across engineering, product, and vendor teams
