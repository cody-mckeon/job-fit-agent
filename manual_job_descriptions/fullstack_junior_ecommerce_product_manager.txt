FullStack Junior Ecommerce Product Manager

FullStack is an AI-native engineering partner built to turn AI capability into production-ready outcomes. The company works with clients through team augmentation and design-and-build product teams.

The role is Junior Ecommerce Product Manager. The product manager will work directly with client teams or FullStack product teams to build and deliver products.

Requirements:
- 2+ years of professional experience in Product Management, Product Ownership, or Digital Product Strategy.
- Proven track record managing backlogs, defining scope, and writing clear user stories.
- Advanced English required.
- Four-year college degree required.
- Experience in e-commerce or digital transaction environments.
- Experience optimizing user journeys, checkout and booking flows, and conversion rates.
- Familiarity with platforms such as Shopify, Magento, or Salesforce Commerce Cloud is a plus.
- Solid understanding of web technologies including HTML/CSS, React, RESTful APIs, performance metrics, and backend integrations.
- Ability to collaborate effectively with engineering teams.
- Willingness to work hybrid onsite 2 days per week in Las Vegas after an initial fully remote period.
- Ability to work through new and difficult issues and contribute to libraries as needed.
- Ability to create and maintain continuous integration and delivery of applications.
- Forensic attention to detail.
- Positive mindset and can-do attitude.
- Experience working on Agile/Scrum teams.
- Meaningful experience working on large, complex systems.
- Extreme ownership over work.
- Ability to identify with client goals and deliver on commitments.

Nice to have:
- AI-enthusiastic workflow.
- Professional experience or personal project work with Cursor, Windsurf, Google Antigravity, Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Codex, or Copilot CLI.

Compensation:
- Base salary range: $80,000 - $90,000.
- Initially remote within the United States, transitioning to hybrid 2 days per week in Las Vegas.
